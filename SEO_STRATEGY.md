# SEO Strategy

## Canonical Structure

- Home: `/`
- Tours index: `/tours`
- Shared flights: `/helicopter-tours/shared-helicopter-flights`
- Main tour pages:
  - `/everest-base-camp-helicopter-tour-nepal`
  - `/annapurna-base-camp-helicopter-tour-nepal`
  - `/muktinath-helicopter-tour-nepal`
- Main service pages:
  - `/helicopter-charter-nepal`
  - `/emergency-helicopter-rescue-nepal`
  - `/pokhara-helicopter-service`
  - `/luxury-helicopter-tour-nepal`
- Destinations:
  - `/destinations`
  - `/destinations/annapurna-region`
  - `/destinations/everest-region`
  - `/destinations/muktinath`
  - `/destinations/pokhara`
  - `/destinations/mustang`
  - `/destinations/langtang`
- Articles:
  - `/blog`
  - `/blog/[slug]`
- Traveler questions: `/faq`

## Rules

- Do not expose duplicate canonical tour URLs in the sitemap. `/tours/[slug]` redirects or canonicalizes where a top-level page exists.
- Do not expose duplicate canonical service URLs in the sitemap. `/services/[slug]` redirects or canonicalizes where a top-level page exists.
- Sitemap `lastModified` uses CMS `updatedAt` for dynamic pages and stable content dates for curated fallback content.
- Metadata must not emit meta keywords.
- Tour pricing should appear only when it has route basis, validity dates, and a recent verification date.
- JSON-LD should describe the actual business, contact details, breadcrumbs, FAQs, and products only when a page has a defensible offer.

## Content Standard

- Avoid unverifiable claims such as guaranteed rescue, best pilots, safest operator, fixed Everest landings, or guaranteed shared departures.
- Use operational language: weather, aircraft availability, permissions, passenger weight, route basis, quote validity, and operator confirmation.
- Keep the white and sky-blue visual identity consistent across public pages.

## Content Clusters

- Pokhara and Annapurna: route planning, seasonal visibility, scenic flights, shared seats, and current quote factors.
- Muktinath: pilgrimage planning, altitude, passenger mobility, weather flexibility, and private versus shared requests.
- Everest: Kathmandu departure planning, altitude and weather questions, landing-basis clarity, and helicopter-versus-trekking decisions.
- Charter: private routing, filming or equipment requirements, corporate travel, quote inclusions, and cancellation terms.

Commercial pages should link to the relevant destination and planning articles. Articles should link back to one closely related commercial page and the availability form. Avoid creating thin location pages solely to repeat keywords.

## Local Authority

- Keep the Pokhara name, address, phone, WhatsApp, and email identical across the site, Google Business Profile, invoices, social profiles, and partner listings.
- Publish genuine office and team photography only after the business supplies it and approves names and roles.
- Ask real hotel, trekking, tourism, and travel partners to link to the most relevant route page where a genuine relationship exists.
- Pursue editorial coverage from Nepal travel publications, Pokhara tourism businesses, destination guides, local news, and customer trip reports. Do not buy link packages, automated guest posts, private blog network links, or mass directory submissions.

## Article Roadmap

Prioritize complete answers rather than publishing all topics at once:

1. Pokhara to Annapurna Base Camp helicopter planning guide
2. What changes an Annapurna helicopter quote
3. Private versus shared helicopter flights in Nepal
4. What happens when a flight changes because of weather
5. Muktinath helicopter planning for families and older travelers
6. Passenger weight and baggage information for Nepal helicopter flights
7. What to wear for a high-altitude helicopter trip
8. Everest helicopter route and altitude questions

Every article needs a named author, publication and update dates, a real or approved route image, internal links, and a practical next step. Do not invent prices, schedules, aircraft, pilot details, certifications, or safety records.
